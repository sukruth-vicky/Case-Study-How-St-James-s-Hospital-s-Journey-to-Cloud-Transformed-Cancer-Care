St James’s Hospital, one of Ireland’s largest national cancer centers, faced major challenges with its legacy infrastructure. 
Outdated systems slowed diagnostic processing, created compliance issues with ISO 15189 standards, and limited the hospital’s capacity to handle the growing volume of genomic data.

To solve these issues, the hospital partnered with EY and Microsoft to modernize its data environment through cloud transformation using Microsoft Azure. 
The goal was to build a secure, scalable, and efficient platform that could improve diagnostics, enhance compliance, and accelerate research.

The collaboration involved the hospital’s scientific teams, EY’s Cloud and Health Transformation group, and Microsoft Ireland. Together, they designed and implemented a cloud-first infrastructure tailored to healthcare needs.

Key steps included migrating data to Azure Cloud, integrating Microsoft Fabric for parallel data processing, and enabling remote access to sequencing tools. 
The new system provided better security, automated data workflows, and improved accessibility for laboratory teams.

The results were significant. Data processing time dropped from nine hours to under two hours, report turnaround time was reduced by half, and testing capacity expanded from 30 to 300 genetic markers. 
The hospital also achieved ISO 15189 compliance, ensuring high standards of accuracy and reliability in diagnostics.

This project demonstrates how cloud technology can transform healthcare operations, creating faster, smarter, and more reliable systems that directly impact patient care.
