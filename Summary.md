St James’s Hospital in Dublin modernized its legacy data systems through a partnership with EY and Microsoft, moving to a cloud-based infrastructure built on Microsoft Azure.

The project improved efficiency, compliance, and collaboration across the hospital’s genomic testing and diagnostics units. Data processing became faster, accuracy increased, and staff gained easier access to tools and reports.

With ISO 15189 compliance achieved and diagnostic speed dramatically improved, the hospital can now handle more samples, deliver quicker results, and provide better patient care.

This case stands as a strong example of how cloud transformation, when aligned with healthcare needs, can lead to meaningful and measurable outcomes.
